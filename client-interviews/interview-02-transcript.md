## Interview Objectives
* To validate the initial requirements provided in the project request email[cite: 3].
* To define the exact roles needed for the Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) implementation[cite: 3].
* To clarify the notification workflow for distributors and inventory staff[cite: 3].

## Key Findings
### 1. Inventory & Alerts
* Large quantities of inventory (Korean ramen, Baskin Robbins) are imported directly, requiring tracking of bulk shipments down to retail distribution[cite: 3].
* **Critical Requirement:** The system must generate automated low-stock alerts via Email and SMS to prevent lost sales[cite: 3].

### 2. Roles and Access (RBAC)
The client confirmed the need for distinct access levels[cite: 3]:
* **Director/Executive:** Requires a remote dashboard for real-time monitoring[cite: 3].
* **Manager/Accountant:** Needs automated financial tools and invoice generation[cite: 3].
* **Inventory Staff & Distributors:** Needs access to stock levels and real-time alerts[cite: 3].

### 3. Infrastructure & Security
* A cloud-based solution is mandatory to serve as a disaster recovery measure, ensuring secure documents and data remain safe if a local failure occurs[cite: 3].

## Notable Client Quotes
> "We need a smart business management system in order to increase the accuracy of company data which affect decisions of directors and authorities... In case a disaster occurs, all secured documents should stay safe and accessible for the company." — Tharidu Dilan[cite: 3]
