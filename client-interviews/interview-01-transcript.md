# Fact-Finding Session: Client Interview #01

**Date:** 2026/05/15
**Interviewee:** Tharidu Dilan, Director - KTD Lanka (Pvt) Ltd.
**Interviewer:** Akila Abenayaka, Avishka Medagamagodage
**Duration:** 2 hours

## Purpose
To gather initial requirements from KTD Lanka for the SmartStream project and establish a baseline understanding of client needs for an integrated business management system[cite: 4].

## Key Topics Discussed
### Current Challenges
* The company relies heavily on disconnected systems and manual Microsoft Excel entries for store and inventory data[cite: 4].
* Data is frequently duplicated, inconsistent, and highly prone to human error[cite: 4].
* Stock shortages are identified too late, leading to lost sales and customer dissatisfaction[cite: 4].
* Manual communication processes (phone calls, emails) between management, staff, and distributors are slow and inefficient[cite: 4].

### Requirements Overview
* **Functional:** A smart inventory management system, automated low-stock alerts (Email/SMS), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), and a remote executive dashboard[cite: 4].
* **Non-Functional:** Secure remote access from any location and cloud-based storage for disaster recovery[cite: 4].

## Key Findings
* **Finding 1:** Manual Excel entry and invoice reviews are the primary bottleneck[cite: 4].
* **Finding 2:** Implementing an automated alert system is a high-priority requirement[cite: 4].
* **Finding 3:** The client strictly requires a robust RBAC implementation to ensure data security[cite: 4].
