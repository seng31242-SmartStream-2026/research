# Structured Interview Guide
**Project:** SmartStream - Integrated Business Management System
**Client:** KTD Lanka (Pvt) Ltd.

## 1. Introduction & Background
* Could you briefly describe KTD Lanka's primary distribution model and the scale of your operations?
* Who are the main users interacting with your current inventory and distribution data on a daily basis?

## 2. Current Workflow & Pain Points
* Walk us through the current process of tracking imported inventory (e.g., Korean ramen, Baskin Robbins)[cite: 2].
* How are you currently using Microsoft Excel for this, and what are its biggest limitations[cite: 2, 4]?
* How do field distributors currently report their deliveries and submit invoices?
* What is the current communication method when stock is running low[cite: 2, 4]?

## 3. Desired System Functionality
* What specific metrics or data points do Directors need to see on a daily basis to make informed decisions?
* How should the system handle physical paper invoices from distributors? (Discussion point: AI OCR integration)
* Do you require real-time location tracking for your field distribution team? (Discussion point: GPS tracking)
* What communication channels do you prefer for automated low-stock alerts[cite: 2]?

## 4. Security, Access, & Infrastructure
* How should access be restricted between Directors, Managers, and Inventory Staff (RBAC)[cite: 2, 3]?
* Do you have any specific requirements for data backup or disaster recovery[cite: 2, 3]?
* Will the users need to access this system remotely from mobile devices[cite: 2]?
